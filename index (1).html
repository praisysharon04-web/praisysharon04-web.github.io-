About

Where ML meets business decisions

My work sits at the intersection of machine learning and business strategy. I'm drawn to problems where a model is only half the answer — the other half is making the result legible to someone who has to act on it. That's shaped how I approach every project: build something technically sound, then build the dashboard or document that lets a non-technical stakeholder use it.

Recent work spans a churn-prediction decision support system, a trajectory prediction pipeline for autonomous vehicles using LiDAR data, and process-mining tooling built with a small team. Alongside the technical track, I've stayed involved in campus leadership and I'm building toward consulting and strategy roles — with an eye on an MBA or MS in Business Analytics down the line.
